About Biringan

A school hidden beyond the mountains, where engineering is taught the way the old stories were told.

About the School

Our university teaches engineering with patience, imagination, and respect for the land. From the first year to graduation, every student is guided by a mentor and joins a community of builders, dreamers, and problem-solvers.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To produce engineers who serve their communities with skill, honesty, and bayanihan spirit.

Our Vision

A nation of engineers who protect the land, the people, and the stories that shaped them.

History

Founded in the spirit of bayanihan, Biringan State University began as a small school of builders. Over the years it grew into a community of five engineering programs, guided by the lessons of Philippine folklore.
