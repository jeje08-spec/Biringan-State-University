Scholarships

Scholarships are awarded based on entrance exam results, financial need, and community service. Ask the Office of Admissions about the Bayanihan Scholarship for students from rural barangays.

FAQ
Do I need to know engineering before I apply?

No. We look for curiosity and problem-solving ability. Your entrance exam and interview show us your potential.

Can I transfer from another school?

Yes. Transfer students are evaluated by the registrar based on completed subjects.

Where do I submit my documents?

Submit them to the Office of Admissions or through the contact form on this site.
