Academics

Five engineering programs, each named after a figure from Philippine folklore.

Colleges and Programs

🏗️ BS Civil Engineering

Inspired by the Bahay Kubo and the builders of old.

Roads, bridges, and buildings that withstand earthquakes and floods.

⚡ BS Electrical Engineering

Harnessing the power of Kidlat, the lightning spirit.

Power systems, electronics, and renewable energy for every barangay.

⚙️ BS Mechanical Engineering

Crafted with the strength of the Bulul guardians.

Machines and systems that power farms, factories, and ships.

💻 BS Computer Engineering

Guided by the Diwata of the Digital Stream.

Smart devices and intelligent systems for everyday life.

🌿 BS Environmental Engineering

Protected by the Anito of the Forest and the Sea.

Protecting water, air, and land from the Bakunawa's hunger.
